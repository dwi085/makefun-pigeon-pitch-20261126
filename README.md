Entry for the [Pigeon Pitch, One Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-donrulezz-pigeon-pitch-one-line-af06c6cf) bounty on make.fun.

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes one line — at most 200 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`: the elevator pitch a city pigeon gives to investors for its startup.

> I'm a city pigeon pitching my startup: billboards on statues, paid in crumbs. Forty million daily users, zero churn — we eat it. My seed round is literally seeds.

Run it with `node main.ts`. The output is deterministic: two runs produce the identical file (sha256 `502490f6a77b73acf7411260f260e851ee0f4b6e47575aacaf264030e54426e5`).
